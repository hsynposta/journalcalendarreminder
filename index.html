Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my journal stored?
On your device. If you enable iCloud Backup in Settings, an encrypted copy is kept in your personal iCloud Drive and synced across your devices.

How do I set a reminder?
Tap the bell while writing an entry, or choose “Add Reminder” from the ⋯ menu on any entry. All reminders are listed in the Reminders tab.

How do I create my own tags?
Tap “+ New Tag” in the editor, or manage tags in Settings → Tags.

How do I export my journal?
Settings → Backup & Sync → “Export All Entries…” creates a file you can save or share.

How do I delete my data?
Delete any entry from its ⋯ menu, or remove the app to erase all local data.
