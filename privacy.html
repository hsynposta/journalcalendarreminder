Daily Journal & Reminder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

The short version

Your journal belongs to you. We do not collect, see, sell or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device — and, only if you choose, in your own iCloud account.

Data storage

All journal entries, photos, documents, locations, moods, tags and reminders are stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud Backup, a copy is stored in your personal iCloud Drive, protected by your Apple ID. The developer has no access to it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. No account or sign-up is required.

Device permissions

The app asks for permissions only when you use the related feature, and the data never leaves your device:

  • Notifications — to deliver the reminders you set.
  • Camera / Photo Library — to attach photos to entries.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — for voice dictation while writing.
  • Location — to attach your current location to an entry when you tap Location.

Data deletion

Delete any entry at any time from its ⋯ menu. Removing the app deletes all locally stored data. You can disable iCloud Backup in Settings at any time.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
